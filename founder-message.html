Sources for the facts in the video

  • 37 million Americans with kidney disease; 87% of adults with it do not know: CDC chronic kidney disease fact sheet, March 2026.
  • DaVita about 36% of U.S. dialysis patients; the two largest chains roughly 70 to 75% together: DaVita Form 10-K, fiscal 2025 and industry analyses.
  • DaVita revenue of $13.6 billion in 2025: DaVita 4th quarter 2025 results.
  • More than $34 million to settle federal kickback allegations in July 2024, without admitting liability: American Bar Association summary of the Justice Department settlement.
  • About one patient in seven dialyzes at home: USRDS 2024 Annual Data Report summary.
  • Early specialist referral and catheter starts, 72 studies: BMC Nephrology 2025 meta-analysis.
  • 83% of the 104 largest patient advocacy groups took industry money; only 1 said it did not: McCoy et al., New England Journal of Medicine, 2017. Details and the full list of sources are on our independence page.
  • American Kidney Fund donor concentration and the whistleblower suit: KFF Health News; AKF's 2025 corporate members.
  • National Kidney Foundation corporate members: NKF 2026 corporate membership program. Dialysis Patient Citizens supporters: DPC supporters page.
  • 3,743 candidates removed from the kidney waiting list because they died in 2024, and 29% of recovered kidneys not transplanted: SRTR 2024 Annual Data Report, kidney.
