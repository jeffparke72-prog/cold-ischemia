Who Pays for the Voice Speaking for Kidney Patients? A Message From the Founder

Who funds the groups that speak for kidney patients? In this message, the founder of the Cold Ischemia Foundation explains why the foundation exists, what the public record shows about industry funding in kidney advocacy, and how an independent organization is different.

IMPORTANT
Taking industry money is legal and common, and many of the organizations named here do real work. Items described as allegations are allegations; DaVita settled without admitting liability, and the American Kidney Fund denies steering patients. If you represent an organization named in this video and believe a statement is inaccurate, write to jeffparke72@gmail.com and we will review it and correct the record where we are wrong.

This video is educational advocacy. It is not medical or legal advice. Talk with your care team about decisions that affect your health.

The Cold Ischemia Foundation is an independent, patient and care-partner led advocacy organization. We accept no donations and no funding from drugmakers, device makers, dialysis organizations, insurers or hospital systems.
