We just released something we do not make lightly: the founding declaration of the Cold Ischemia Foundation.

It is not an advertisement. It is not a fundraiser. It is not a feel-good message meant to be watched and forgotten. It is a record, and I recorded it so there is evidence of what we saw, what we said, and what we promised to do about it. If we ever fall short of these words, play this back to us and hold us to it. That is the point.

Why that name? In transplant medicine, cold ischemia is the window in which something living can survive after it has been cut off from what sustains it. We chose it because we have watched the same thing happen to people. Not to organs. To human beings, cut off from answers, from respect, and from any say in their own lives, and kept alive while slowly going cold.

It rarely happens all at once. A voice is surrendered in installments: a question waved away, fear answered with a pamphlet, a concern charted as non-compliance, a care partner sent to the hallway while an entire life is decided behind a closed door. Until one day the person who once asked, argued, and fought simply goes quiet.

Here is what I most want you to hear. Your silence was never your failure. It was engineered. Exhaustion is persuasive and despair is patient, and too many people have been worn down by a system that is relieved when they stop asking.

So the Foundation was built on seven principles we are willing to be measured by:

1. Your experience is evidence. Your questions are data.
2. Document everything you are told and everything you are promised, so no one can rewrite it later.
3. Data belongs to the person it describes, not to the institution that profits from it.
4. A system should be judged by how fast it listens.
5. Care partners belong in the record as experts, not as tolerated visitors.
6. Technology should hold the line when every office is closed and every phone goes unanswered.
7. Advocacy should not ask for a seat at the table. It should audit the table itself.

We are not asking for permission. We are building what the system declined to build and measuring what it refused to measure. We will publish what we learn, correct what we get wrong, and answer to the people we serve.

If you are one of the people who has gone quiet, this is your invitation back. Your voice was never the problem. It was waiting for a place where it would be heard, and that place begins now.

Watch the whole declaration here, share it with someone who truly needs to hear it tonight, and join us at coldischemia.foundation.

With us, no one waits in the cold alone.
