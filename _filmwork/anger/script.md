# THE COLD TRUTH ABOUT "INDEPENDENT" KIDNEY ADVOCACY
Voiceover script, about 3 minutes 35 seconds at 155 words per minute, or about 4 minutes at a measured 140. No names of individuals.

## 1. THE CHAIR (0:00 to 0:35)

I know the chair.

I know the cold of it. The four hours that stop being yours.

I know what it is to be handed a pamphlet when what you needed was an answer. And I know the look on a face when someone finally realizes that nobody is coming to explain.

They told you this was a disease you manage. They never told you it was a market you are inside.

I am not here to comfort you. Comfort is what they sell. I am here to show you the receipts.

## 2. THE PROMISE (0:35 to 1:00)

In 1972, Congress promised to pay for dialysis for anyone whose kidneys failed. Fifty-four years later, thirty-seven million Americans have kidney disease, and the CDC says eighty-seven percent of adults who have it do not know.

Eighty-seven percent. That is not a diagnosis gap. That is a business model with a waiting room.

## 3. THE DUOPOLY (1:00 to 1:45)

Two companies, DaVita and Fresenius, treat roughly three in four dialysis patients in this country.

DaVita alone booked thirteen point six billion dollars in 2025. In 2024 it paid more than thirty-four million to settle federal kickback allegations, without admitting liability. Those are allegations. The revenue is not.

Only about one patient in seven dialyzes at home. A 2025 analysis of seventy-two studies found early specialist referral cut catheter starts by more than half. Most people still hear their options after the crisis, not before.

Prevention does not bill by the treatment.

## 4. THE FRIENDLY FACES (1:45 to 2:35)

Now, the organizations that say they speak for you.

KFF Health News reported that the American Kidney Fund drew eighty-two percent of its 2018 funding from just two companies, the two biggest dialysis chains. The fund denies steering patients, and the Justice Department declined to join the whistleblower suit. But you are allowed to ask who pays for a charity's independence.

The National Kidney Foundation lists drug makers on the top tier of its corporate membership, and its own policy says it must remain independent.

I am not claiming a sponsor writes the script. I am saying that when the people who profit from your treatment fund the voices that comfort you, "independent" needs an audit.

They all promise innovation. Tools. Equity. A new era. What you get is a webinar.

## 5. THE WASTE (2:35 to 3:10)

Transplant is no better. In 2024, three thousand seven hundred and forty-three people were removed from the kidney waiting list because they died. Another four thousand nine hundred and one because they became too sick.

That same year, twenty-nine point three percent of recovered kidneys were never transplanted. In a decade-long comparison, France discarded nine percent when we discarded eighteen. We built a system that throws away what it begs for.

For more than two decades, a race-based formula made Black patients look healthier than they were and pushed them back in line. Fixing it took until 2023, and the first year alone needed more than fourteen thousand seven hundred waiting-time adjustments.

In 2025, the government moved to decertify an organ procurement organization for the first time ever.

## 6. THE ANSWER (3:10 to 3:35)

That is why the Cold Ischemia Foundation exists.

We take no pharmaceutical money. No dialysis-industry money. We answer to patients and care partners, because they are the only ones who ever paid the full price.

We build the tools, the guides, and the mentors so you understand your own body, your own numbers, and your own choices before the system makes them for you.

You have choices. No one who profits from your silence will tell you that.

We will.
