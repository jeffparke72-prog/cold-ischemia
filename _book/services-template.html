Priced for where you live

Your price follows your cost of living.

A family in a low-cost area should not pay what a family in a high-cost city pays. So every base price is scaled to your area: your price = base price × your area’s cost-of-living index ÷ 100. We use the U.S. Bureau of Economic Analysis Regional Price Parities (RPP), where 100 is the national average.

Index values are from BEA’s published Regional Price Parities (2022–2023 releases; the year is shown with each state). Do not see your state or city? Look up your state or metro area on the BEA table and choose “My area”, or just tell us your ZIP code and we will confirm the index and quote your price in writing. Prices are rounded to the nearest dollar.

Who we serve

  • Legislators, staff and public agencies

Who we don’t work for

  • Drug and device makers
  • Dialysis companies
  • Insurers and payers
  • Hospital and transplant-program systems whose practices are at issue
  • Advocacy organizations funded by any of the above

That is how independence works in practice: no one who profits from patients’ care buys our time. Paying clients never get editorial control.

Our independence pledge

We do not accept donations. We take no funding of any kind from drug, biologic, medical-device or pharmaceutical companies, dialysis organizations, insurers, hospital systems, or any advocacy organization funded by them. We charge fees only for services you request, at posted rates, and fees never buy influence over what we research or say.

Questions

Straight answers.

Are you lawyers, doctors or financial advisors?

No. We are advocates, writers and researchers. Nothing we provide is medical, legal, tax or financial advice. Letters and briefs are advocacy documents you review and sign. For medical advice, talk to your healthcare team, and in an emergency call 911.

Do paying clients get a say in what you publish?

No. Fees pay for our time on your request. They never buy influence over our research, our conclusions or what we say about anyone.

How is my price set?

We start from a base price and scale it to your local cost of living using the U.S. Bureau of Economic Analysis index for your state or metro area. A family in a lower-cost state pays less than the base; a family in a higher-cost area pays somewhat more. We confirm the number and quote it in writing before any paid work starts.

What if I can’t pay?

Ask. Say so in your request and we will review it. The free tools, guides and one orientation call are always available.

How do I get started?

Send a request through the contact page. Tell us what you need and the deadline. We reply with what is free, what would cost, and a written quote before any paid work starts.
