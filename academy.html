The courses

Take them in order for the full picture, or start where you need help most. Your progress is saved on this device, and you can pick up exactly where you left off.

How it works

No instructor, no schedule, no cost. Built for patients, care partners, clinicians, students, reporters and anyone who wants to understand the system well enough to change it.

Watch and listen

Jeff presents every scene, with captions on by default. Pause, rewind, or change the speed any time.

Do, don't just watch

Estimate the real numbers, make the call in real scenarios, put steps in order, and flip cards for the tools.

Check every fact

Every module lists its sources, from CDC and MedPAC to court opinions and federal rules. Open them yourself.

Earn a certificate

Pass each course's final assessment at 70% or better to print your certificate of completion.
