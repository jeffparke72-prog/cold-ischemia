# Appendix B: A Short Glossary

**Allocation.** The rules that decide in what order candidates are offered an organ.

**Cold ischemia time.** The interval between the cooling of an organ in the donor and the restoration of blood flow in the recipient.

**Confidence interval.** A range of values consistent with the data; a wide range means the study learned little.

**Control chart.** A plot of a metric over time with statistical limits that separate ordinary variation from a true signal.

**CTQ (critical to quality).** A requirement the customer cares about, stated in a form that can be measured.

**DCD (donation after circulatory death).** Donation after death is declared on the basis of the permanent stopping of the heart and circulation.

**Delayed graft function.** A transplanted kidney that does not work promptly, usually defined as the need for dialysis in the first week.

**DMAIC.** Define, measure, analyze, improve, control: the five phases of a Six Sigma improvement project.

**DPMO.** Defects per million opportunities, the unit used to compare the performance of different processes.

**Donation service area.** The geographic territory assigned to a single organ procurement organization.

**Fishbone diagram.** A cause-and-effect diagram, introduced by Kaoru Ishikawa, that groups possible causes of a problem by category.

**Gauge R&R.** A study that tests whether a measurement process gives the same answer when repeated by the same person and when performed by different people.

**Hazard ratio.** The ratio of the rate of an event in one group to the rate in another; a value of 1.013 per hour means a 1.3% higher rate for each added hour.

**HBOC (hemoglobin-based oxygen carrier).** A cell-free preparation of hemoglobin designed to carry and release oxygen.

**Hypothermic machine perfusion.** Continuous pumping of cold preservation solution through an organ.

**Kappa.** A statistic that expresses the agreement between raters beyond what chance would produce.

**Nonuse.** The registry's term for a recovered organ that is not transplanted.

**Normothermic perfusion.** Perfusion of an organ at or near body temperature.

**OPO (organ procurement organization).** A designated organization responsible for recovering organs from deceased donors in a defined area.

**OPTN (Organ Procurement and Transplantation Network).** The national network that sets policy for organ allocation.

**Pareto chart.** A ranked bar chart that shows which categories account for most of a problem.

**Rolled throughput yield.** The proportion of units that pass every stage of a multi-stage process without a defect; the product of the stage yields.

**SIPOC.** A one-page map of a process listing suppliers, inputs, process steps, outputs, and customers.

**Sigma level.** A conventional measure of process capability; higher values correspond to fewer defects.

**SRTR (Scientific Registry of Transplant Recipients).** The registry that analyzes transplant outcomes.

**Voice of the customer.** The statement of what the customer needs, gathered directly and translated into measurable requirements.
