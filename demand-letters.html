Formal Correspondence — On the Record

This Letter Does Not Ask. It Demands.

On March 30, 2026, Cold Ischemia Foundation sent formal demand letters to Congress, the Florida Legislature, HHS, CMS, and NIH. In June 2026, CIF submitted a formal Lean Six Sigma methodology directly to the OPTN Board of Directors. Every office was given thirty days to respond in writing. Non-response is being treated as a matter of public record.

The Letters
United States Congress & Leadership
March 30, 2026
RE: Federal Compensation Mandate for Unpaid Family Care Partners, All Chronic Disease Categories
"I am a transplant recipient who is alive because someone cared for me without compensation, without recognition, and without the support of a single federal or state program designed for their benefit. That person's labor — and the labor of 53 million Americans like her — will no longer be treated as a free resource available for governmental exploitation."

Dr. Jay Bhattacharya, Director, NIH
March 30, 2026
RE: Research Priority Designation for Care Partner Outcomes
"Less than 3% of NIH chronic disease research funding is directed toward caregiver outcomes — despite peer-reviewed evidence that caregiver burden directly determines recipient mortality, medication adherence, and hospitalization rates. Gold-standard science does not ignore the most consequential unmeasured variable in its primary area of study."
