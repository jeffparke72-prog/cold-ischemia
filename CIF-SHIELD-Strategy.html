The Three Root Causes
  • Root Cause 1: CMS excludes care labor from all reimbursement models. No federal classification of "family caregiver" exists in statute.
  • Root Cause 2: Industry-captured patient advocacy organizations redirect reform energy toward pharmaceutical revenue protection rather than patient welfare.
  • Root Cause 3: No federal statute grants legal standing, compensation access, or healthcare rights to family caregivers in chronic illness or transplant settings.

Pareto chart confirms 80% of documented harm traces to these three causes. Fishbone diagram published in CIF white paper and submitted with congressional testimony package.

About the Cold Ischemia Foundation

Built by people who lived it.
Run with the rigor of people
who refuse to lose.

"We are not a patient advocacy organization. We are a structural accountability organization. The difference is everything."

The Cold Ischemia Foundation was co-founded by Jeff and Marie Parke in Ellenton, Florida — Congressional District FL-16. It emerged from three kidney transplants, a radiation oncology nursing career, and the recognition that the system depending on family caregivers has never once acknowledged they exist in federal law.

CIF operates with zero pharmaceutical, dialysis, or insurance industry funding. This is not a marketing position. It is a founding constraint that cannot be waived — because the organizations that accept industry funding are precisely what CIF was created to expose.

The SHIELD Method is the product of a Lean Six Sigma Green Belt background applied to healthcare systemic failure. It is rigorous, reproducible, and designed to withstand congressional testimony, peer review, and public challenge.

CIF does not wait to be invited to the table. It builds the table. Its work has been submitted to the U.S. Senate, U.S. House of Representatives, HHS, CMS, NIH, DOL, and the Florida Legislature.

CIF's Core Legislative & Accountability Tools
Marie's Lifeline Compensation Act
Federal legislation establishing compensation, legal standing, and healthcare access for family caregivers in chronic illness and transplant settings. Named for Marie Parke. Advancing through congressional channels.
Renal Patient Protection & Medical Information Integrity Act
Addresses information asymmetry, algorithmic bias in payer AI denial systems, and patient rights protections in renal and transplant care settings. Drafted for congressional intake.
True Advocacy Assessment
Live public scoring system for patient advocacy organizations — evaluating pharmaceutical funding transparency, industry independence, and genuine advocacy output. Active at surveys.coldischemia.foundation.
40 Federal Violations Audit
A fully cited audit of more than forty documented federal regulatory and statutory violations across CMS, HHS, NIH, DOL, and the Florida Legislature. Filed formally. On public record. Cannot be unseen.
