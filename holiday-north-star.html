Find Your North Star

Every right patients have today exists because someone, very often a tired family member, refused to be quiet. Those people are still up there. Light them one by one. Then add a star of your own.

  1. 1Touch a faint star to light it
  2. 2Finish a constellation to name it
  3. 3Answer four questions and draw yours
Part one

The sky that was already there

Eleven stars, eleven real moments. None of these people were paid to show up. Most of them were sick, or exhausted from caring for someone who was. Each one is sourced, so you can check it and quote it.

Your star goes here
How to read this sky

Start anywhere. Every star is a person who would not stay quiet.

  1. Touch or tab to a faint star. Its story opens here.
  2. Light every star in a group and the lines draw themselves: Speaking up, Organizing, Writing it down, Changing the rule.
  3. The empty space in the middle is saved for you. Part two, below, fills it.
0 of 11 stars lit
What every star had in common

Not a title. A set of habits.

Nobody in this sky was handed the word "advocate." They earned it by doing a few unglamorous things over and over, usually for free, usually while tired.

They wrote it down

Dates, names, exact words, lab values. The waiting time that came back in 2023 came back because records proved it.

They asked the specific question

Not "why is this happening to us," but "which policy are you following, and may I see it?"

They knew the written rule

A grievance right you can quote by its number is very hard to brush aside.

They kept the patient's voice in the room

Shep Glazer did not send a summary. He came himself, connected to the machine.

They organized

The 504 sit-in lasted 26 days because neighbors, churches and a union kept bringing food.

Nobody was paying them

Independence is why they could say the uncomfortable thing and come back the next day to say it again.
