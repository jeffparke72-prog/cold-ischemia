# A Note Before We Begin
EPIGRAPH: "Felix qui potuit rerum cognoscere causas."
BY: Virgil, Georgics II.490 ("Fortunate is the one who has been able to learn the causes of things.")

A few plain words about how this book was made, because a book that asks you to trust it owes you the details.

**The scenes are composites.** When a chapter opens with a picture of a person or a room, it is built from documented patterns, not from an individual. I have given no one a real name, and I have invented no statistics inside any scene. Whenever I describe an actual person or event, I cite the source.

**The facts are graded.** Every claim carries an inline citation. Behind each one is a rating I assigned in the source notes at the back. A means a primary document, a registry, or a peer-reviewed paper whose key figures I could confirm. B means a credible secondary account. C means a figure from a single source or from a party with a stake in the result, such as a manufacturer or an advocacy campaign. Material graded C is always labeled as preliminary where it appears.

**Where I could not see the whole page.** The research tools I used returned summaries and bibliographic details, not the full text of every article or agency page. A dagger (†) after a citation means the central figures were confirmed but some detail, such as a complete author list, should be completed from the original before any future edition. I would rather show you that gap than hide it.

**The date.** This was written in October 2026. I have leaned on developments from the preceding six months wherever they exist, and I say so when the newest figure is older. A few landmark studies appear as background and are labeled as such.

The Foundation publishes educational material about oxygen-carrier technology, including the work of a company called BHOC Therapeutics. The author reports no financial interest in that company or in any other organization discussed here. The Foundation's own position is that the technology is a research concept whose benefits are unproven, and this book repeats that position without softening it.
