# EVIDENCE LEDGER v2 — "What Time Is For" (compiled 9 Oct 2026 from search summaries; full texts not reachable)
Grades: A primary/registry/peer-reviewed with figures confirmed; B credible secondary; C single-source, manufacturer or advocacy; † bibliographic details partly confirmed.
(v1 ledger in ../_paper/ledger.md still applies to: registry counts, nonuse, hep C, biopsy, center acceptance, DIVAT, Freiburg, HRSA/KYDA, Life Alliance, AP opt-outs, HOLD Act, EKDA, OPO tiers, IOTA, HMP/NMP/HEMO2life, MDR, Part B-ID, OBBBA.)

## Time and the dialysis chair
- RECOVERY (A†) Kidney Medicine 2020 (Alvarez et al.): online survey of 359 in-center patients; median recovery 3 h (range 0–24); 40% ≥4 h; fatigue/washed out 62%, cramps 44%, low-BP symptoms 42%. Overview: typical median 2–4 h; ~25% report >6 h (review).
- SONGHD (A†) "Developing a set of core outcomes for trials in hemodialysis: An international Delphi survey" (2017): 1,181 participants round 1 (202 patients/caregivers, 979 professionals), 73 countries; 838 (71%) completed round 3. Patients/caregivers rated higher: ability to travel (+0.9), dialysis-free time (+0.5), dialysis adequacy (+0.3), washed out after dialysis (+0.2). Professionals rated higher: mortality, hospitalization, BP decrease, vascular access complications, depression, cardiovascular disease. Inclusion: median ≥8 and ≥75% critical. Final core set not confirmed by me.
- DEPRESS (A†) Seminars in Dialysis 2025 (doi 10.1111/sdi.70043): 137 cross-sectional studies, 21,608 HD patients; pooled depression 45.8% (95% CI 42.0–49.6); high-income countries 38.9%. Other reviews: ranges 5.5–76%; earlier meta-analyses 20–40%; anxiety 15–30%.
- ISOLATION (A†) PMC12825595: population-based cohort of CKD patients: most socially isolated group 33% higher mortality; loneliness group 10% higher; life expectancy reduced 2.04 y (F)/2.98 y (M) most isolated; 0.58 y (F)/1.03 y (M) lonely. Not dialysis-specific. Reviews: treatment schedules dominate social life; social support protective.
- CAREGIVER (B†) Several cross-sectional studies (Indonesia 2022 IJERPH; Greece; India 2025 abstract): hours of care associated with depression, anxiety and strain; small samples.

## Little platoons
- LDC (B) Live Donor Champion (Johns Hopkins): six monthly ~1-h sessions; candidate picks friend/family/community advocate. Pilot: 15 candidates vs 15 matched controls — 25 potential donors contacted, 4 participants achieved living-donor transplant, 3 more in evaluation, 0 among controls (p<0.001). Larger: 163 candidates, 81 donor referrals, 5.5-fold higher odds of ≥1 referral vs matched controls (conference abstract). Single-group implementation study (111 enrolled, NCT03435510). No randomized trial found.
- MATNAT (B†) Israel faith-based organization (PMC5992728): 494 live kidney donations Feb 2009–end 2017; retrospective.
- SPAIN (A/B) Ministerio de Sanidad 2026 release: 2025 = 51.9 deceased donors pmp; 2,547 deceased donors; 408 living donors; ~6,335 transplants (129 pmp). 2024 record: 52.6 pmp, 2,562 donors; 132.8 transplants pmp (La Moncloa, Jan 2025). Model: national, regional and hospital coordination; hospital coordinators mostly anaesthesiologists/intensivists, often part-time. Cantabria >100 pmp 2025; six other regions >60.

## The quiet option
- CKM (A†) CKDopps 2023 (Kidney Medicine): of 1,018 people with CKD, 7% planning conservative management; of 407 surveyed, 18% had a conversation about forgoing dialysis; no provider had a dedicated CKM clinic or protocol.
- CKM-VA (B†) VA Boston qualitative study, 20 nephrologists: guidelines recommend discussing conservative management at stage 4–5 but rarely happens; emotional strain.
- CKM-ROCH (B†) Rochester case series, 13 patients: choices shaped by values and prior dialysis experience.
- CKM-OBS (B†) Observational: age ≥75 with comorbidity/functional impairment — survival and QoL may not differ appreciably; dialysis associated with more hospitalization, less dying at home/hospice; patients willing to give up meaningful life expectancy to reduce burden (editorial).
- CKM-JAMANO (B†) JAMA Network Open 2026 (doi 10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2026.36332): qualitative study from two US randomized trials (2019–2024) on why older adults choose CKM; abstract only.
- WITHDRAW (B†) Withdrawal reported as cause of death in 20–30% of ESKD patients in Western countries (likely underestimated). Midwestern tertiary centre 2001–2015: 1,226 patients, 536 died, 262 (49% of deaths) withdrew; death forms ~73% sensitive. After withdrawal survival typically about a week (Australian abstract; Italian program <7 days for most; fewer hospitalizations, more home deaths).

## Upstream
- CDC (A) CDC page updated March 2026: 14% of adults (37 million) estimated to have CKD. Earlier CDC analysis (via Becker's): ~30M/15%; 96% of early-stage unaware; 48% of severely reduced function unaware and not on dialysis. NKF Aug 2025: 35.5M; 9 in 10 unaware (underlying calc not found). 2024-era study of CKD G3–G4: about half unaware.
- UACR (B) Median albuminuria testing among adults with type 2 diabetes in primary care across 24 organizations: 52.9% (2021 study cited in FLOW commentary).
- FLOW (A†) FLOW trial (Nat Med 2024; PMC11485243): first dedicated kidney-outcomes trial of a GLP-1 RA (semaglutide 1.0 mg) in T2D+CKD; benefit regardless of concomitant SGLT2i (550 with, 2,983 without at baseline); ADA Standards recommend SGLT2i first-line for most adults with T2D and CKD. No real-world uptake rate found.
- PREEMPT (B†) Preemptive transplant uncommon: 17% of recipients overall and 31% of living-donor recipients (2003–2012 analysis); 2.5% of patients with incident kidney failure in 2015 received preemptive transplant as first therapy; 2025 UNOS study: mortality benefit of early preemptive transplant debated.
- WAITMOD (A/B) OPTN Policy 3.7.D effective 5 Jan 2023: backdate waiting time for Black candidates whose race-inclusive eGFR delayed qualifying (race-neutral eGFR ≤20). By 5 Jul 2023: 6,103 complete modifications, median 1.7 years awarded; share of Black candidates transplanted before dialysis 11.7% → 13.9% (preliminary). JAMA Intern Med (reported Mar 2026): >181,000 adults; >21,000 candidates received added time Jan 2023–Jun 2025; +5.3 transplants per 1,000 listings among Black candidates, both pre-dialysis and post-dialysis groups.

## Home and device
- HOME (B/C) Home share: incident patients 6.8% (2010) → 13.3% (2020) (USRDS-based study); market data 31 Mar 2025: ~433,400 in-center, ~78,400 home (≈15% by my arithmetic; not official); home HD 2.4% of ~550,000 prevalent dialysis patients (2022, manufacturer-cited); NxStage >13,000 HHD (2023). Old federal target: 80% of new patients on home dialysis or transplant by 2025 (>85% then in centers; older article).
- QUANTA (C) FDA 510(k) clearance for home use of Quanta system Nov 2024; Diality Moda-flx cleared for facility use, home indication pending.
- ETC3 (B) CMS ETC third evaluation (Aug 2025; CY2021–2023): increase in home dialysis training and overall transplant rates (deceased-donor driven); summaries also say no detectable increase in home dialysis rates, waitlisting, or transplant rates — apparent conflict; no impact on Medicare spending or quality. 95 HRRs, 2,591 facilities, 141,890 FFS beneficiaries (34%). First-year: +546 patient-months of home training (+9%); +225 transplants (+10%). Proposed to end 31 Dec 2025 (final decision not confirmed).
- WAK (A†) Wearable artificial kidney, JCI Insight (2016): well tolerated, effective clearance; trial (NCT02280005) stopped after seventh subject for device-related technical problems; redesign needed.
- KIDNEYPROJECT (B/C) UCSF bioreactor survived in pig without immune attack (2023); a 2026-dated university page says still in animal testing, human trials estimated 2026–2027 (unverified).

## Distance and workforce
- CHADRON (B) KFF Health News, 15 Apr 2026: rural Nebraska hospital unit closed; 17 patients incl. Pieper; one driving >4 h round trip; another living in a weekday rental.
- NKF2023 (B) NKF Oct 2023 letter: ~170 facilities closed within a year (staffing, supply chain, costs); cites 2023 study: median one-way distance increase 6.6 miles (IQR 0–20.5) in small-town/rural areas (older data).
- CLOSURE (B†) Registry-based closure study (2001–2015 data): closures in remote areas associated with more hospitalizations, missed treatments, marginal mortality increase; more likely to affect Black and Medicaid patients.
- RURALBRIEF (B) SC Rural Health Research Center 2017: rural facilities more often low-volume and negative-margin; fewer stations, fewer late shifts.
- WORKFORCE (B) Rural health commentary (Sept 2026): adult nephrology fellowship fill 94.1% (2010) → 65.8% (2024); applicants −37%; HRSA projection of 21% nephrologist shortage by 2037 (via commentary); rural 30 vs urban 263 physicians and specialists per 100,000 (all specialties). Healio 2025: appointments +13% for July 2025, fill 73%. USRDS-based: 240,000 rural ESKD patients have less nephrology access and higher mortality. Tele-nephrology case report: >100 patients in 12 months.

## Gift
- NKR (B) National Kidney Registry: 10,000th living-donor transplant; 1,744 transplants in 2024; expected ≈30% of all US living-donor kidney transplants in 2025; record month 179 transplants (Oct, likely 2025) vs 157 (Jul 2024); 104 member centers. Chain #124 (2008): 30 transplants, 17 hospitals, 11 states, four months. NEJM 2009 chain: 10 transplants, six hospitals, five states, eight months, nobody reneged. UAB chain: described as 74 donors/74 recipients, later 101 recipients; current total unconfirmed; sources disagree on definitions.
- DONORFAM (B†) Australian interview study of 49 potential donor families: no consenting family regretted; some non-consenting did; process stressful but not enough to reduce satisfaction. Review: dissatisfaction with hospital staff more likely than donation itself to worsen grief; privacy, pressure, fear of not honoring wishes aggravate. 15-country European inventory: half thought aftercare could improve; anonymous written contact possible in almost all countries, direct contact in few. Sque et al. (conference abstract): 1,613 letters over seven years; recipients wrote more and usually first; donor families wrote about the donor, recipients about gratitude. Southern California OPO: consent rose after family-services/aftercare department created in 2002 (abstract; causation not established).

## Innovation (new)
- ANDREWS (A/B) Tim Andrews (eGenesis EGEN-2784, MGH): pig kidney Jan 2025; 271 days without dialysis; removed Oct 2025 for progressive dysfunction; human kidney Jan 2026 (first to progress from pig to human kidney); early T-cell-mediated rejection resolved; no pig pathogen transmission; published in The Lancet 3 Sep 2026; Nature Medicine immune profiling (doi 10.1038/s41591-025-04053-3). eGenesis Sept 2026 update (company): five expanded-access patients; three sustained function >8 months without dialysis; one >9 months (record); two transitioned to human kidneys. FDA cleared UKidney IND Feb 2025 and EGEN-2784 IND Sept 2025; EXPAND first transplant NYU 3 Nov 2025. AJT "Xenotransplantation in living human recipients: Where we stand in 2026."
- MDR101 (A/B) Medeor MDR-101 phase 3: 20 treated, 10 control; 2023 interim: 16 of 19 off immunosuppression at two years; July 2025 AJT: ~95% discontinued all immunosuppression ~1 year; 75% immunosuppression-free >2 years; four resumed (recurrent IgA nephropathy, rejection); none developed GVHD. April 2026 interview (Leventhal): Treg + stem-cell phase 1b; randomized phase 2 of single-agent immunosuppression.
- GRAFT (A†) AJT 2021 SRTR analysis: median graft survival deceased-donor 8.2 y (1995–1999) → ~11.7 y (recent); living-donor 12.1 y → ~19.2 y (2014–2017). ATC abstract: 5-yr survival DD 67.0→72.9%, LD 80.2→84.7%; 10-yr DD 45.1→51.5%, LD 61.5→68.0% (eras 1995–99 to 2005–09).

## Transport and allocation
- TRANSPORT (A/B) HRSA organ transport page: FAA new website 2025 for air transport of organs incl. cabin guidance; HRSA working group final report May 2025, 20 recommendations. McDermott+ 2024: current contract cannot require organs to be tracked. UNOS tracking tool used by 16 OPOs. HRSA two-year monitoring report on 2021 kidney allocation change: kidneys travel farther, most within 250 nautical miles; nonuse 21% → 26% post-policy. Continuous distribution of kidneys: status "paused" (Kidney Committee, 28 Jul 2025); no 2026 update found.
