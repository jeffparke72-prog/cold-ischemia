What This Certificate Is.
And What It Is Not.

This program awards a Certificate of Completion issued by the Cold Ischemia Foundation. We believe in transparency — so here is exactly what that means.

What You Receive
  • ✓A Certificate of Completion from the Cold Ischemia Foundation — signed, dated, printable, and shareable.
  • ✓Demonstrated mastery of care partner advocacy, federal regulatory frameworks, transplant medicine policy, pharmaceutical capture analysis, and structural change methodology — verified through 72 quiz questions and a 25-question final examination.
  • ✓A policy education that most credentialed healthcare professionals have never received. The content in this program is not taught in nursing school, medical school, or any standard healthcare administration curriculum.
  • ✓The analytical framework, regulatory citations, and advocacy tools to walk into a congressional office, a CMS comment period, or a transplant center meeting — and be taken seriously.
What This Is Not
  • —This is not an accredited certification. It is not approved by ANCC, ACPE, ACNM, or any professional accrediting body. We are a grassroots advocacy foundation, not an academic institution.
  • —This program does not award continuing education credits (CEUs, CME, CNE, or equivalent). It cannot be submitted to a licensing board for renewal requirements.
  • —This is not a substitute for legal or clinical training. It is policy education for advocates — not a license to practice law, medicine, or any regulated profession.
