The Cold Ischemia Foundation accepts zero dollars from pharmaceutical companies, dialysis corporations, medical device manufacturers, or hospital systems. If that is you — or if you believe care partners deserve real tools and real independence — this is where that belief becomes action.

Why we rely on private donations — and why that matters.

Every major patient advocacy organization in kidney care and chronic illness has taken pharmaceutical money. Some disclose it in fine print. Most do not lead with it. The result is an entire advocacy ecosystem that cannot credibly challenge the industry funding it. Policy positions get softened. Accountability reporting disappears. The organizations that claim to represent patients end up representing the financial interests of their sponsors.
