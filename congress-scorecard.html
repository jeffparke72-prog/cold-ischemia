Methodology

Scores are calculated from documented votes, co-sponsorship records, and committee actions on legislation directly affecting ESRD patients, transplant access, organ procurement, and caregiver compensation. Absences are scored as 0. Active opposition votes are scored as negative. Co-sponsorship of care partner or kidney legislation adds points independent of final passage vote.

Scores reflect the 119th Congress (2025–present) and selected legacy votes from the 117th–118th Congress where they remain material to current policy. This is not a partisan document. Grades are determined entirely by voting record. Sources: Congress.gov, GovTrack, Senate and House Clerk roll call records.
