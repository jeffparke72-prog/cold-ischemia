Questions

How fast does a new release reach the feed and my calendar?

This calendar updates the second a release opens. The feed is rebuilt automatically every hour and whenever new material is published, so feed readers and email services receive it within the hour. Calendar subscriptions show every scheduled release in advance, with an alert at opening time.

Why can't I open a release early?

Each release opens at a set time so that everyone, including patients, families, reporters and the companies named, sees it at the same moment. Past releases never close.

What does "In verification" mean?

An installment of The Inside Files is published only after the editor has checked its claims against the primary documents. When that takes longer than a week, the calendar says so plainly instead of counting down to a date we cannot promise.

Do you collect my email or track me?

No. The calendar subscription, the feed and the email service all work without giving us anything. Your "read" checkmarks live only in your own browser.

Can I share a release?

Yes. Every release has its own permanent link. Open it from this calendar and share the address from your browser.
