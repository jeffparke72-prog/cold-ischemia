THE FIELD STATION

Eight evidence-based methods for high-tension home dynamics. Select the situation. Surface the method. Use the phrases — in the moment.

Based on the chapter When the Air Changes: Psychological Fieldcraft for High-Tension Home Dynamics.
Methods drawn from mentalization research (Camoirano, 2017), trauma-informed practice (Sweeney et al., 2018), conflict mediation (Howieson, 2024), and expressed-emotion frameworks (Fahrer et al., 2022).
