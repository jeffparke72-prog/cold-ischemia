Pharma FundingDec 2023KFF Health News & Public Citizen
$6 Billion in Pharma Grants Flowed to Patient Advocacy Groups Between 2010 and 2022
Public Citizen's landmark analysis documented that PhRMA and 31 member companies provided at least $6 billion in grants to more than 20,000 organizations over 12 years — dwarfing their federal lobbying and campaign contributions combined. Organizations receiving these funds were significantly less likely to support drug pricing reform legislation that would benefit their own patient constituencies.
Read the full analysis →
CIF Position
Cold Ischemia Foundation accepts zero pharmaceutical funding by founding constitutional constraint — not policy preference. This is why we can say what organizations receiving this money cannot.
Fraud & EnforcementSept 2025HHS Investigation / KSTP
HHS Investigation: At Least 28 Patients May Not Have Been Deceased at Time of Organ Procurement
An HHS investigation into an OPO serving Kentucky, Ohio, and West Virginia found at least 28 patients may not have been deceased at the time of organ preparation, with 73 patients showing neurological signs incompatible with donation between December 2024 and February 2025. CMS announced "major reforms" including an independent oversight board and a direct patient reporting channel.
Read the investigation →
CIF Position
CIF's 40 Documented Federal Violations audit addresses exactly these structural OPO oversight failures. A direct reporting channel is not reform — it is the minimum accountability floor that should have existed from day one.
Organ TransplantJan 2025UNOS / OPTN
U.S. Surpassed 48,000 Organ Transplants in 2024 — Fifth Consecutive Record Year
Despite a fifth consecutive record year, 104,000 Americans remain on the waiting list and 17 people die daily. UNOS is calling on Congress to mandate automated donor referrals, in-cabin airline organ transport, and a national organ tracking system.

Pfizer, Novo Nordisk, and AstraZeneca poured €110 million into patient advocacy organizations across Europe in 2022 alone. One researcher summarized the mechanism: "Patient groups are better marketing managers than pharma companies because they are seen as neutral or objective."

Transplant ScienceMar 2026Global Observatory on Donation & Transplantation
Record 173,727 Solid Organ Transplants Performed Worldwide in 2024
The Global Observatory on Donation and Transplantation's 2024 report documents a record 173,727 solid organ transplants worldwide — a 2% global increase driven largely by donations after circulatory death, which accounted for 28% of all deceased donation activity. Major challenges persist including continued organ shortage and pronounced geographic disparities in transplant access.
