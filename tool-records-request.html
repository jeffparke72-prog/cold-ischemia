Medical Records Request Generator

Why This Tool Exists

Access to medical records is one of the most important rights patients and care partners have. Accurate records are necessary for second opinions, treatment planning, insurance appeals, and documenting events when concerns arise within healthcare systems.

Unfortunately, many families experience delays or incomplete responses when requesting records. Requests may be ignored, partially fulfilled, or delayed for weeks. This tool helps organize a clear and structured request so the provider understands exactly what records are being requested.

Under federal law, patients generally have the right to access their medical records. Submitting a clear written request often speeds up the process and reduces confusion about what documentation is required.
