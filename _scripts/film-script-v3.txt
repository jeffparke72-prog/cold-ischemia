COLD ISCHEMIA FOUNDATION: "THE CLOCK DOESN'T KNOW YOUR NAME"
Film script v3, 3:00. Voices: narrator (mature male), founder (male), volunteer.


== THE MIND AT 3 A.M. ==
0:01  NARRATOR        At three in the morning, the mind does arithmetic.
0:05  NARRATOR        How long does a kidney last? How long do I?
0:10  NARRATOR        How many names are ahead of mine?
0:14  NARRATOR        You were told to be patient. To be compliant. To trust the process.
0:19  NARRATOR        So you became a very good patient. You filled out every form. You kept the phone close.
0:26  NARRATOR        And underneath it all, one quiet question. Is the process thinking about me at all?

== WHAT THE BROCHURES LEAVE OUT ==
0:37  NARRATOR        Here is what the brochures leave out.
0:40  NARRATOR        Seventeen people die every day waiting for an organ.
0:46  NARRATOR        Not only from disease. From delay. From a form in the wrong stack. From a decision made in a room they were never invited into.
0:56  NARRATOR        The rules exist. The conduct does not follow. And almost no one with power is asking why.

== ELLENTON, FLORIDA, 9:40 PM: THE CONVERSATION ==
1:07  VOLUNTEER       I almost didn't come.
1:10  VOLUNTEER       I'm tired. I'm sick of being a case number. And I don't know what one person can even do.
1:18  JEFF (FOUNDER)  One person is exactly how this started.
1:22  VOLUNTEER       Against all of that?
1:24  JEFF (FOUNDER)  Read the page everyone else skimmed. Ask the question nobody wanted asked. Put a name where the system put a number.
1:32  VOLUNTEER       And when they ignore you?
1:34  JEFF (FOUNDER)  Then we write it down. Dates. Names. Decisions. A record doesn't get tired. A record doesn't get scared.
1:41  VOLUNTEER       Who pays for this?
1:43  JEFF (FOUNDER)  Nobody who profits from the answer. No drug money. No dialysis money. No hospital money. That is the only reason you can trust a word we say.
1:54  VOLUNTEER       That sounds lonely.
1:56  JEFF (FOUNDER)  It was. Until tonight.

== WHAT WE LEAVE BEHIND ==
2:02  NARRATOR        Everyone leaves something behind.

2:10  NARRATOR        You cannot choose how long the clock runs. You can choose what you do while it runs.
