Who We Are

Built by a recipient.
Inspired by his care partner.

Jeff Parke is a three-time kidney transplant recipient and clinical research professional. Marie Parke is the care partner who held everything together through three transplants — legally required, never compensated, never protected. Together they built the platform that was missing.

Not because they wanted to. Because it did not exist.

"We take no pharmaceutical money. We name the institutions. We close the loop — not with a wellness score, but with a filed complaint and a federal record the institution is legally required to respond to."

Jeff Parke · Executive Director · Cold Ischemia Foundation
