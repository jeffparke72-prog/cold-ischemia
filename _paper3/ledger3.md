## The promise (history)
- GLAZER (B) Shep Glazer, a patient/salesman (age ~43), was dialyzed before the House Ways and Means Committee, Nov 1971 (sources differ on exact date and on his organization). 
- LAW (A/B) Social Security Amendments of 1972, P.L. 92-603, signed 30 Oct 1972; Medicare extended to people of any age needing dialysis or transplant; program began 1 July 1973.
- COST (B) Experts expected ~10,000 patients and ~$135 million (sources differ on whether annual). 2012 USRDS: 525,481 Medicare ESRD beneficiaries, >$28.6 billion/yr. 2025 ADR: Medicare ESRD spending $55.3 billion in 2023; adjusted prevalence 2,327 per million.
- 2023ADR (A) Per-person traditional Medicare cost 2021: hemodialysis $99,325; PD $86,976; functioning transplant $43,913. Waitlisted share of prevalent dialysis patients 12.3% (2021). ESRD ≈1% of Medicare beneficiaries, ≈7% of FFS spending (ASN letter 2024, B).
- Well-known history from general knowledge, to be confirmed from primary texts (flagged †): Kolff rotating-drum machine (Netherlands, 1943); Scribner shunt (Seattle, 1960; Clyde Shields first patient, lived 11 more years); Seattle patient-selection committee, Life magazine 1962; Herrick identical-twin transplant, Boston, 23 Dec 1954 (Joseph Murray, Nobel 1990).

## The body
- SEXDYS (B) Meta-analysis cited in trade summary: sexual dysfunction prevalence 71.2% across CKD; 77.6% hemodialysis; 76.7% predialysis; 56.9% transplant; linked to depression and worse quality of life. Women's data thin (21 meta-analyses in men, 2 in women, per one review); single studies vary 25–76% in women.
- PREG (A†) Hladunewich et al., J Am Soc Nephrol 2014: Toronto intensive HD 22 pregnancies, live birth 86.4%, median 36 weeks; US registry cohort 70 pregnancies, 61.4%, median 27 weeks; ≤20 h/wk 48% vs >36 h/wk 85%. Small, retrospective.
- LANG (B) Person-first language movement since 1974; Temple Health "Our Words Matter"; Home Dialysis Central blog on "non-compliant"; vignette experiments (not nephrology) show stigmatizing wording raises blame.

## Heat, place and people
- HEAT (A†/B) 2024 nationwide study: 945,251 dialysis patients, 245 US cities, deaths up 15–20% during extreme humid-heat events; highest in the Southeast. Heat and missed sessions: open question.
- AIAN (B/C) American Kidney Fund: AI/AN kidney failure ~2x white Americans (source year unconfirmed). CDC Vital Signs (Jan 2017, A): diabetes-related ESRD incidence among AI/AN fell 54% 1996–2013 and equaled whites among adults with diabetes by 2013; diabetes causes 69% of new ESRD in AI/AN vs 44% overall; credited to Indian Health Service diabetes program from the mid-1980s.
- CROWD (A†/B) Canadian transplant GoFundMe study (PLoS ONE 2019): 258 kidney campaigns; kidney campaigns raised 11.5% of goals; liver nearly half. US: nearly 20,000 organ campaigns, Black and Hispanic campaigners raised less (secondhand). Abstract of 1,324 campaigns: average raised $10,701 vs goal $30,365.
