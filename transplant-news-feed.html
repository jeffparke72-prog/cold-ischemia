Add a source any time

  1. Open the source list on GitHub.
  2. Copy any line, paste it below itself, and change the name, url (any RSS or Atom feed), and category (Policy, Research, Journals, or News).
  3. For general news feeds, add "filter": true so only transplant and kidney articles are kept.
  4. Click Commit changes. The new source appears within minutes.

Or just tell Claude the feed address, and it will add and verify it for you.
