Care Partner Cognitive Stabilization Engine

Authority Under Strain™

AI-Powered · Cognitive Stabilization · Strategic Escalation · Institutional Communication

There are moments in chronic illness care when the cognitive load becomes so heavy — the grief, the exhaustion, the institutional pressure, the fear — that your ability to think and act from a position of authority begins to fracture. This is the tool for those moments. Authority Under Strain stabilizes your decision-making capacity and generates the communications you need to be heard by the systems that are failing you.

How This Tool Works
You will move through four modules. First you will describe your current situation and your cognitive state. Second you will identify the institution and the specific failure you are responding to. Third you will document your evidence and the stakes involved. Fourth the AI generates two outputs — a Stabilization Brief that restores your clarity and authority, and a formal Escalation Communication ready to send.

Your responses are processed in real time and are never stored. This tool requires your Anthropic API key to generate outputs.

Module 04 of 04 · AI Stabilization Brief + Escalation Communication
Two Outputs Will Be Generated
Output 1 — Stabilization Brief. A private document for you alone. The AI will analyze your cognitive state, name what is happening to your decision-making authority, and deliver specific stabilization language and a strategic priority sequence for the next 24–72 hours. This is not advice. It is a mirror.

Output 2 — Escalation Communication. A formal, professionally structured letter ready to be sent, faxed, or delivered to the institution and recipient you identified. It will cite your evidence, name the failure specifically, state your demand with a response deadline, and reference applicable accountability mechanisms.
