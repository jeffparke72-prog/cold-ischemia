Care Partner Rights
With patient authorization, care partners have right to access records, receive information, and be present during care discussions. Facilities cannot deny access without documented clinical justification.
Key CFR Citation
45 CFR §164.524 (access to records)
45 CFR §164.510 (care partner disclosures)
Key Rights Under 42 CFR §494.70
Right to be informed · Right to participate in care planning · Right to file grievance without retaliation · Right to adequate notice before discharge · Right to access records
Legally Enforceable
Discharge Protections — 42 CFR §494.180
Facility cannot discharge without adequate written notice. Must ensure continuity of care during transition. Discharge cannot be used as retaliation for exercising rights.
Enforce Via
1. Your ESRD Network (grievance)
2. Your State Survey Agency
3. CMS Regional Office
4. OIG (if retaliation involved)
