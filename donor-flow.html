Living donors aren't missing.
They're being lost.

Every 24 hours, 17 Americans die waiting for a kidney. More than 80% of people who say yes to living donation never reach the operating table — not because they're disqualified, but because the system that's supposed to carry them through evaluation was never built to hold on to them. Project Donor-Flow is Cold Ischemia Foundation's eight-stage AI architecture for closing that gap, grounded in peer-reviewed evidence at every stage.
