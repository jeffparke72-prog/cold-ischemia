Based on Project Donor-Flow's research into why living donors are lost during evaluation, this guide walks through the real categories that matter — clinical, psychosocial, financial, logistical — with a real, working link to a genuine resource for each one.

This is an educational discussion guide, not a clinical or diagnostic tool. It does not assess your eligibility, predict graft outcomes, or replace evaluation by a transplant center. Check items to build a list of questions and resources to bring to your own transplant team.
