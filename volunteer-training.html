Before you speak for someone,
learn what they're living.

This is the foundation's self-led readiness training. Ten modules. One hundred questions. No time limit and no shortcuts. It teaches what the work really asks of you, then asks you, honestly, whether you have the time, the commitment, and the judgment to do it well. Every answer you give helps protect a patient or care partner you haven't met yet.
