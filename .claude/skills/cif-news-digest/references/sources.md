# Where to look, and how to search

## Journals worth scanning (specialty journals surface what mainstream misses)
Kidney International; Kidney International Reports; Journal of the American Society of Nephrology (JASN); Clinical Journal of the American Society of Nephrology (CJASN); American Journal of Kidney Diseases (AJKD); Kidney Medicine; Kidney360; American Journal of Transplantation; Transplantation; Transplant International; Clinical Transplantation; JAMA Network Open; JAMA Internal Medicine; Health Affairs; BMJ; The Lancet; NEJM (for policy-grade pieces).

## Registries, agencies, oversight (Tier 2)
OPTN data and policy notices; SRTR program reports; USRDS Annual Data Report; CMS rules and ESRD Quality Incentive Program notices; Federal Register (look for proposed rules with open comment periods); HHS Office of Inspector General; Government Accountability Office; MedPAC reports; HRSA notices.

## Search patterns
PubMed (use date filter for the window):
- ("Kidney Transplantation"[MeSH] OR "Living Donors"[MeSH]) AND caregivers
- "Renal Dialysis"[MeSH] AND (ownership OR "for-profit")
- "Kidney Transplantation"[MeSH] AND (disparities OR access OR waitlist*)
- ("Patient Advocacy"[MeSH]) AND ("conflict of interest" OR "industry funding")
- "living kidney donor" AND (outcomes OR "financial" OR "long-term")
Web search fallback: add the year and the words "journal article" or "PubMed". Cross-check any claim against at least two independent results (publisher page, PubMed, PMC) before stating it.

## Quick quality filters
- Reject if no author names, no journal, or no identifier.
- Reject retracted papers and expressions of concern.
- Prefer open-access items so patients can read the source themselves.
- Check for predatory-journal red flags (no editorial board, vague peer review, unsolicited fees).
