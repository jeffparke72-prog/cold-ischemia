What happens when you call

1. Ask for the right person
Say you want to become a living kidney donor and ask for the living donor coordinator or the transplant social worker. If they are not available, ask for the transplant program’s administrative office.
2. Ask what happens first
Ask whether screening can start by phone or online, how long evaluation takes, and who your independent living donor advocate will be.
3. Ask about your costs before you travel
The recipient’s insurance usually covers your medical evaluation. Ask whether tests can be done near home and visits bundled, and about help with travel and lost wages.
