How to use these tools

In the guide. In the room.
On your phone.

Built for three contexts — all three work without an account.

01
While reading the guide
Each chapter with a companion tool includes a QR code in the margin. Scan it from your phone while reading. The tool opens immediately — no app, no login required.
02
In the moment
Bookmark this page. When a high-tension dynamic begins, open it, select the situation, and let the tool surface the method. The phrase library gives you exact language to use.
03
After the exchange
The pattern naming and OODA tools are built for reflective use after a difficult interaction — to identify what happened and interrupt the same cycle next time.
04
Share with others
This page is public. Share the URL directly with a social worker, family member, or care partner you know. No account is required to access anything here.
About this resource

Built for the care partners
the system forgot to train.

This tools hub is a standalone companion to the Survival Guide for Care Partners in High-Tension Home Dynamics — an evidence-informed field guide covering volatility, coercion, guilt, verbal aggression, and psychological erosion in caregiving.

The tools here address dimensions of caregiving that clinical systems almost never touch: what to do with the air in the room, not just the medications in the drawer.

The Cold Ischemia Foundation is a grassroots structural advocacy organization for chronically ill patients and care partners. No pharmaceutical funding. No institutional compromise.
